Use  in Kubernetes when you want high-priority pods to be scheduled first but not evict existing, lower-priority pods. It is used for batch jobs, debug/diagnostic tools, or non-critical services that should wait for resources to become available naturally rather than disrupting running applications. 

• Key Use Cases: 

	• Batch Jobs/Non-critical Services: High-priority batch tasks that should not disrupt critical production workloads. 
	• Debug/Diagnostic Pods: Temporary troubleshooting pods that should not interfere with active traffic. 
	• Resource Management: When you want to ensure a pod is scheduled before others in the queue, but you want to maintain stability by avoiding disruption. 

• Behavior: 

	• Pods with  will stay in the scheduling queue until sufficient resources are free. 
	• They are still scheduled ahead of lower-priority pending pods. 
	• They cannot kill existing lower-priority pods, unlike the default .
