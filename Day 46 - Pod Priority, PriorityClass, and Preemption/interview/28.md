#### Scenario 1: A Pod is stuck in "Pending" state. What are the possible reasons?
**Detailed Troubleshooting Path:**
1.  **Check Events:** `kubectl describe pod <pod-name>` - Look at the **Events** section at the bottom.
2.  **Reason A - Insufficient Resources:** The message says `0/3 nodes are available: insufficient cpu`. **Fix:** Scale nodes (Cluster Autoscaler) or lower the Pod's `resources.requests`.
3.  **Reason B - No Matching Node Selector/Affinity:** Message says `node selector mismatch`. **Fix:** Label a node correctly or remove the selector from the pod spec.
4.  **Reason C - Taints & Tolerations:** The nodes have taints (e.g., `NoSchedule`), but the pod has no matching toleration. **Fix:** Add toleration to pod spec or remove taint from node.
5.  **Reason D - PVC Binding:** If the pod uses a PersistentVolumeClaim (PVC) that is waiting for a PersistentVolume (PV) to be available. **Fix:** Check PVC status with `kubectl get pvc`.
