## Pods are scheduled on nodes that do not meet application requirements.

### Troubleshooting Steps:

```bash
# Step 1: Verify node labels
kubectl get nodes --show-labels

# Step 2: Update pod affinity rules
nodeSelector:
  disktype: ssd

# Step 3: Drain unintended nodes
kubectl drain <node-name> --ignore-daemonsets --force

# Step 4: Reschedule pods
kubectl rollout restart deployment <deployment-name>
```
