## Pod Scheduling with Preferred Node Affinity - You have three worker nodes, `worker-2` and `worker-3`. When you deploy a container, it must **prefer** `worker-2` as its first choice. Only if `worker-2` is unavailable should it schedule on `worker-3`.

**Solution:** Use a `preferredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution` affinity rule.

1.  **Apply a common label** to the target nodes (e.g., `region=india`) to make them a group.
2.  **Apply a unique label** to the preferred node (e.g., `zone=power-a`).
3.  **Use a `required` rule** to ensure the pod stays within the group (India region).
4.  **Use a `preferred` rule** with a higher weight to give first preference to a specific zone.

**Example Manifest:**
```yaml
apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: demo
spec:
  replicas: 1
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: demo
  template:
    metadata:
      labels:
        app: demo
    spec:
      affinity:
        nodeAffinity:
          # Rule 1: MUST be in the India region
          requiredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution:
            nodeSelectorTerms:
            - matchExpressions:
              - key: region
                operator: In
                values:
                - india
          # Rule 2: PREFER the power-a zone
          preferredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution:
          - weight: 100
            preference:
              matchExpressions:
              - key: zone
                operator: In
                values:
                - power-a
      containers:
      - name: nginx
        image: nginx
```
