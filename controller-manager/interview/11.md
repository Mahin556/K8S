## Question 7: Will a Deployment Work Without a Selector?

**Scenario:** If you remove the `selector` section from a Deployment YAML, will it work?

**Answer:** **NO** - Deployment will NOT work without a selector.

**However:**
- **ReplicationController (RC)** CAN auto-derive selectors from pod template labels
- **Deployment** and **ReplicaSet** REQUIRE explicit selectors

This is a common CKAD exam question - the manifest might have the selector missing, and you need to add it.
