### Explain the use of Labels and Selectors in Kubernetes.

## Labels and Selectors in Kubernetes

Labels and Selectors are used in Kubernetes to organize, identify, and connect resources such as Pods, Deployments, and Services.

---

## Labels

Labels are key-value pairs attached to Kubernetes objects.

They are used to identify and categorize resources.

### Example Label

```yaml id="5m2p8"
labels:
  app: nginx
  env: production
```

Here:

* `app=nginx`
* `env=production`

---

## Selectors

Selectors are used to find and match resources based on labels.

They connect:

* Services to Pods
* Deployments to Pods

### Example Selector

```yaml id="8k1n4"
selector:
  app: nginx
```

This selector matches Pods having the label:

```yaml id="3x7q2"
app: nginx
```

---

## How They Work Together

```text id="6v1m5"
Deployment → Labels Pods
Service → Uses Selector to Find Pods
```

---

## Benefits

* Resource organization
* Easy grouping and filtering
* Service-to-Pod communication
* Simplified management

---

### Simple Interview Answer

“In Kubernetes, Labels are key-value pairs used to identify and organize resources, while Selectors are used to match those labels and connect resources like Services, Deployments, and Pods.”
