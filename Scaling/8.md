Good question — jab ek hi direction (scaleUp ya scaleDown) mein **2 ya zyada policies** ek saath di jati hain, to HPA **dono policies calculate karta hai aur fir `selectPolicy` ke hisab se ek result chunta hai.**

## Step 1: selectPolicy samjho (default = "Max")

Tumne `selectPolicy` specify nahi kiya, to **default `Max`** use hoga. Iska matlab:

> HPA dono policies se allowed replica change calculate karega, aur **jo zyada aggressive (bada) change allow kare**, uska use karega.

(Agar `selectPolicy: Min` hota, to **chhota** change wala policy use hota. `Disabled` se wo direction completely band ho jata.)

## Step 2: dono policies kya allow karti hain

```yaml
policies:
- type: Percent
  value: 50
  periodSeconds: 60      # current replicas ka 50% badha sakta hai, per 60s
- type: Pods
  value: 5
  periodSeconds: 60      # ya max 5 pods badha sakta hai, per 60s
```

Har evaluation pe, **current replica count** ke basis pe dono ka allowed increase calculate hota hai, fir bigger wala chuna jata hai.

## Detailed example (current replicas badalte hue)

Maan lo metrics ke hisab se HPA **bahut zyada replicas** chahta hai (jaise 50+), to ye dekho actual replicas kya hoga based on current count:

| Current replicas | Percent policy (50% of current) | Pods policy (fixed +5) | Konsa bada hai? | Final allowed increase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 1 (50% of 2 = 1) | 5 | **Pods wins** | +5 → **7** |
| 4 | 2 | 5 | **Pods wins** | +5 → **9** |
| 8 | 4 | 5 | **Pods wins** | +5 → **13** |
| 10 | 5 | 5 | **Equal** | +5 → **15** |
| 14 | 7 | 5 | **Percent wins** | +7 → **21** |
| 20 | 10 | 5 | **Percent wins** | +10 → **30** |
| 40 | 20 | 5 | **Percent wins** | +20 → **60** |

## Yahan se pattern dikhta hai

- **Jab replicas kam hain** (jaise 2-9): Percent policy ka absolute number bahut chhota hota hai (50% of 4 = sirf 2). Wahan **Pods policy (+5) jeet jati hai** — taki kam replicas hone par bhi reasonable speed se scale ho sake.
- **Jab replicas zyada hain** (jaise 14+): Pods policy fix +5 hi deti hai, jo bade scale pe slow lagta hai. Wahan **Percent policy jeet jati hai** — taki bade deployment proportionally fast scale ho.
- **Crossover point** yahan **10 replicas** pe hai, kyunki `50% of 10 = 5` — dono equal ho jate hain.

Formula: crossover = `value_pods / (value_percent / 100)` → `5 / 0.5 = 10`.

## Kyun ye combination use karte hain (real-world logic)

Ye exact pattern Kubernetes ke **default scaleUp behavior** mein bhi use hota hai (Percent 100% + Pods 4), kyunki:

- **Sirf Percent use karte** to: agar tumhare paas 2 replicas hain, 50% = 1 pod — bahut slow scale-up jab load achanak spike ho.
- **Sirf Pods use karte** to: agar tumhare paas 100 replicas hain, +5 fixed bahut slow lagega bade scale pe — proportionally kuch nahi.

Dono ko `Max` selectPolicy ke saath combine karne se — **chote scale pe fast absolute growth**, aur **bade scale pe fast proportional growth** — dono mil jata hai. Best of both worlds.
